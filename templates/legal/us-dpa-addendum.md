# ApexMail US DPA Addendum

**Status:** DRAFT, not effective and not offered to customers until the owner approves it.
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-15
**Version:** 0.1 (draft for owner/counsel review)
**Owner approval:** not recorded, `approved_by` and `approved_at` are unset on purpose. An agent never approves a legal document: the owner (or counsel instructed by the owner) approves it, and only then does this file lose its draft status.

This draft is an addendum to the ApexMail [Data Processing Agreement](dpa.md) ("DPA") for customers established in the United States of America ("US Customer"). It reflects the processing roles under US state privacy laws (California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, and comparable state statutes) and keeps the GDPR transfer terms for any EU personal data a US Customer sends through the Service.

Draft review notes for the owner:

- The clause set, the conflict order and the signature block are proposals, not advice.
- Bracketed items are decisions only the owner can make: state-law coverage, insurance amounts, and whether a US-specific service-provider annex is offered at all.
- Nothing here changes the shipped product. Where the Service has a control (EU-only routing, suppression handling, retention classes), the clause refers to that control truthfully.

## 1. Parties and Scope

### 1.1 Parties

- **Customer**: the entity identified in the signature block, established in the United States ("US Customer").
- **Processor / Service Provider**: **Bel Consulting OÜ** (trading as **ApexMail**), registry code **16588745**, VAT **EE102951727**, registered address **Sakala 7-2, 10141 Tallinn, Estonia**, Republic of Estonia ("ApexMail").

### 1.2 Scope

This addendum applies where:

- the Customer is established in the United States, and
- ApexMail processes Personal Data on the Customer's behalf in providing the Service.

It does not replace the DPA. It supplements the DPA for US state privacy law purposes and preserves the DPA's GDPR terms for EU personal data that a US Customer submits to the Service.

### 1.3 Relationship to the DPA

The DPA remains the base agreement. The DPA's terms on processing instructions, confidentiality, security measures, data subject rights assistance, deletion and return, and audit survive and apply here. This addendum adds the US service-provider framing and the US-specific operating terms.

## 2. Definitions

Capitalized terms not defined in this addendum have the meaning given in the DPA, in the applicable US state privacy law, or in GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679).

- **"CCPA"** means the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018 as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020, including its implementing regulations.
- **"Consumer"**, **"Business"**, **"Personal Information"**, **"Sensitive Personal Information"**, **"Sell"**, **"Share"**, **"Targeted Advertising"**, **"Cross-Context Behavioral Advertising"** and **"Service Provider"** have the meanings given in the CCPA.
- **"Process"** and **"Processing"** mean any operation performed on Personal Data, consistent with both the CCPA definition for service providers and GDPR Article 4.
- **"State Privacy Laws"** means the CCPA and the other US state privacy statutes that the parties agree apply to the Service, as listed by the owner in the coverage note of the signed version of this addendum.
- **"EU Personal Data"** means Personal Data whose Processing is subject to GDPR, including because the data subject is in the European Economic Area.

## 3. Roles

### 3.1 Service Provider Role

For Personal Information the Customer submits to the Service, the Customer is the Business and ApexMail is the Service Provider. ApexMail Processes the Personal Information only on the Customer's documented instructions, which include the Agreement, the DPA, this addendum, and the Customer's configuration of the Service.

### 3.2 No Sale, No Sharing, No Targeted Advertising

ApexMail shall not:

- Sell or Share the Personal Information;
- Process the Personal Information for Targeted Advertising or Cross-Context Behavioral Advertising;
- retain, use or disclose the Personal Information outside the direct business relationship between the parties, except as permitted for service providers by law;
- combine the Personal Information with personal information ApexMail receives from or on behalf of another person, except as permitted by the CCPA for a service provider.

### 3.3 No Independent Use

ApexMail shall not Process the Personal Information to build or improve products or services it offers to others, to train models it sells, or for any commercial purpose other than the Service. The Service's own platform operations (deliverability, abuse prevention, security, billing) count as providing the Service and are permitted.

### 3.4 Limited Use of Sensitive Personal Information

Where the Customer submits Sensitive Personal Information, ApexMail Processes it only to provide the Service, and applies the security measures in Annex 1 of the DPA. ApexMail does not use Sensitive Personal Information to infer characteristics about a Consumer.

## 4. Service Provider Obligations

ApexMail shall:

1. comply with the applicable obligations of State Privacy Laws for Service Providers and provide the same level of privacy protection to Personal Information as the CCPA requires of a Business;
2. notify the Customer if it determines that it can no longer meet those obligations;
3. allow the Customer to take reasonable and appropriate steps to stop and remediate unauthorized use of Personal Information;
4. engage subcontractors only under a written contract that imposes obligations no less protective than this addendum and the DPA, and remain liable for their performance;
5. make available the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this addendum, consistent with the audit clause in Section 10;
6. not respond to a Consumer request directly, except as instructed by the Customer or required by law, and in either case notify the Customer.

## 5. Customer Obligations

The Customer shall:

- provide the Personal Information lawfully, including any notices and consents its Business obligations require;
- issue processing instructions that are lawful and complete, and keep the recipient data in the Service accurate;
- not instruct ApexMail to Process Personal Information in a way that would cause ApexMail to Sell or Share it, or to Process it for the Customer's own Targeted Advertising where that would fall outside the Service Provider role.

## 6. Consumer Rights

### 6.1 Assistance

Taking into account the nature of the Processing, ApexMail assists the Customer in responding to Consumer requests to know, access, correct, delete, or opt out of Sale or Sharing, using the Service's suppression, export, retention and deletion tooling.

### 6.2 Referral and Notification

ApexMail refers to the Customer any request it receives directly from a Consumer and does not act on it except on documented instructions, or where law requires otherwise. Where law requires direct action, ApexMail notifies the Customer promptly afterwards, unless prohibited.

### 6.3 Opt-Out Signals

The Service honors suppression and unsubscribe records and the DPA's instructions on communicating opt-outs. Where the Customer relies on an opt-out preference signal, the Customer is responsible for passing the resulting instruction to the Service; ApexMail is responsible for enforcing suppression records once recorded.

## 7. EU Personal Data Sent by a US Customer

### 7.1 GDPR Applies to That Data

Where the Customer sends EU Personal Data through the Service, the DPA's GDPR terms apply to that data, including the Data Processing Agreement's controller and processor framing, Article 28 obligations, and the DPA's transfer safeguards. This addendum does not dilute them.

### 7.2 Transfers

Where EU Personal Data is transferred to or accessed from a third country, ApexMail relies on the DPA's transfer mechanisms, which include the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, Module 2 (controller to processor), Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914, incorporated by reference, and adequacy decisions where they apply.

### 7.3 Government Requests

ApexMail handles government requests for EU Personal Data as set out in the DPA's government request clause: prompt notification of the Customer unless prohibited, challenge of unlawful or overbroad requests, and disclosure of the minimum necessary.

### 7.4 Residency Controls

The Service offers EU-only routing, which keeps a tenant's mail on ApexMail's own European relay and holds a message rather than sending it through a non-EU transport. A US Customer that requires that control can select it; the routing mode, its holds, and the EU-only status are recorded in the Service and are auditable.

## 8. Security and Breach Notification

### 8.1 Security Measures

ApexMail maintains the technical and organizational measures in Annex 1 of the DPA, reviewed under the DPA's change and review clauses.

### 8.2 Breach Notification

ApexMail notifies the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the Customer's Personal Information. The notification:

- describes the nature of the breach and the categories and approximate number of Consumers and records concerned;
- identifies a contact point for information;
- describes the likely consequences and the measures taken or proposed;
- is provided in phases where all information is not available at once, without undue further delay.

### 8.3 Statutory Notifications

ApexMail supports the Customer's own notification duties under State Privacy Laws and applicable US state breach notification statutes by providing the information the Customer needs, without making admissions of fault or liability.

## 9. Subprocessors and the Generated Register

The current subprocessors are listed in the ApexMail Subprocessor Register at https://apexmail.ee/subprocessors/, which forms part of this addendum. That register is generated from ApexMail's deployment-derived subprocessor facts (the same generator that renders the mirror in `templates/compliance/subprocessors.md`), so it names the real processing locations and the transfer safeguard for each subprocessor. New subprocessors are added under the DPA's notification and objection mechanics.

## 10. Audit and Assessment

The DPA's audit clause governs. In addition, once per twelve-month period the Customer may request ApexMail's then-current security questionnaire responses, control mapping documentation, and penetration test summary, where available, as an assessment under State Privacy Laws. Assessments are limited to the processing facilities and systems relevant to the Service and are conducted under confidentiality.

## 11. Deletion and Return

The DPA's deletion and return clause governs:

- the Customer may export Personal Data within 30 days after termination, using the Service's export tools;
- ApexMail deletes remaining copies through its erasure pipeline, with statutory retention or suppression records kept only where keeping them is required or necessary to enforce suppression;
- copies in backups become inaccessible through encrypted backup rotation.

ApexMail does not perform physical media sanitization, and this addendum does not promise any.

## 12. Liability

Each party's liability under this addendum is subject to the liability provisions of the Terms of Service. A breach of a State Privacy Law obligation owed by the Customer as a Business remains the Customer's responsibility. Nothing in this addendum limits rights a Consumer has under State Privacy Laws.

## 13. Order of Precedence

If the DPA's GDPR terms and this addendum conflict, the following order applies to the extent of the conflict:

1. mandatory law of the relevant jurisdiction prevails over both;
2. this addendum prevails over the DPA for matters governed by State Privacy Laws (service provider role, Consumer rights assistance, and US breach support);
3. the DPA prevails for matters governed by GDPR, including the transfer safeguards and residency commitments;
4. the source that is more protective of the individual Data Subject or Consumer prevails over one that is less protective, where the conflict survives steps 1 to 3.
