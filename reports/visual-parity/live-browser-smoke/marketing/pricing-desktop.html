Frequently Asked Questions

How does ApexMail pricing work?

We charge based on your monthly email volume with no hidden fees. Every plan includes all features — DKIM, SPF, DMARC, webhooks, and analytics. You only pay for what you send.

Is there a free tier?

Yes! We offer a generous free tier of 10,000 emails per month. No credit card required. You can upgrade at any time as your volume grows.

What happens if I exceed my plan limit?

We never stop your emails. If you exceed your plan's volume, you'll be billed at the pay-as-you-go rate for the overage. You'll receive alerts at 80% and 100% of your limit.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes — annual billing saves you 20% compared to monthly. All plans are available with annual billing. Contact our sales team for custom enterprise agreements.

Is HIPAA compliance included?

HIPAA compliance is available as an add-on for $199/mo. It includes a signed BAA, encrypted storage at rest, and audit logging with 7-year retention. Available on any paid plan.

Can I switch plans at any time?

Absolutely. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from your dashboard. Changes take effect immediately and billing is prorated. No lock-in contracts, no cancellation fees.
