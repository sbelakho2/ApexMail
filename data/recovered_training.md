# Recovered Corpus Retired

The historical recovered JSONL corpus was removed with the broader unsupported model-training and autonomous-agent data retirement.

It was not a trustworthy source for future model work: records included unverified synthetic account data, unsupported tool execution, and stale product and delivery claims. It is not retained as a backfill source and must not be restored without the governed model program described in [the training-retirement note](../apps/ai/training/README.md).
