Governance

Compliance

Legal policy is a hard gate, not a warning: every decision resolves the recipient's jurisdiction against the approved policy before anything is sent. A jurisdiction that is unknown, unlisted, or below the country-confidence floor blocks the decision.

Blocked decisions and their block reasons appear under Exceptions. An unapproved jurisdiction cannot send — there is no override on this page or in the control API's review flow.
