Sovereignty

European by construction.

ApexMail is built in Europe and its sovereignty features are evidence-linked, not adjectives. Global delivery is the default; EU-only routing is the setting that removes the third-party transport.

Sovereignty facts and their evidence.
FactDetailEvidence
An EU company, under EU lawBel Consulting OÜ, Tallinn (registry 16588745): Estonian governing law, Harju County Court.Contracts and DPA
EU data centresFinnish data centres; core service data is processed and replicated inside the EEA.Data location matrix
EU-only routing, fail-closedOne setting sends exclusively through ApexMail's own EU MTAs; no third-party transport, and a send with no own-MTA route is held, not rerouted.Pricing and availability

Scope

The Global default delivers through AWS SES in its EU regions, which may include Germany, EU regions of a US company; ApexMail's own hosting is in Finland. EU-only routing removes that transport; the full comparison is on the sovereignty page.
