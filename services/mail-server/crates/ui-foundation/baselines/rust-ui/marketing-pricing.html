Frequently Asked Questions

How does ApexMail pricing work?

We charge based on monthly email volume with clear plan limits. DKIM, SPF, DMARC, webhooks, and core analytics are included broadly; higher tiers add dedicated IPs, SSO, audit logs, compliance workflows, and account coverage.

Is there a free tier?

Yes. We offer a free tier of 30,000 emails per month. No credit card required. You can upgrade at any time as your volume grows.

What happens if I exceed my plan limit?

Where overage is enabled for a subscription, extra email volume is billed at €0.40 per 1,000 emails. Usage, abuse, and payment controls continue to apply. Review your billing setup for the applicable limits and alerts.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes. Annual subscriptions are billed at 10 times the monthly price, which is roughly a 17% discount versus twelve monthly payments. Enterprise annual contracts follow the signed order form.

Is HIPAA compliance included?

HIPAA availability is not currently offered. Enterprise can support compliance workflow discussions, but a regulated use case may proceed only after written confirmation and any required agreement with our security and legal teams.

Can I switch plans at any time?

Paid-plan changes use secure Stripe Checkout or the Stripe billing portal. A plan does not change until Stripe confirms the billing event and ApexMail reconciles the verified Stripe webhook. Enterprise annual contracts follow the signed order form and service terms.
