Billing cycle

Press Calculate, your estimate renders instantly on the next page using the billing service's live plan tables.

Plan boundaries are drawn from the active billing catalog: Free (3,000/mo), Developer (50K/mo), Pro (150K/mo), Growth (500K/mo), Business (2M/mo), Enterprise Cloud (5M/mo). Dedicated IPs are an add-on from €49/month (first) and €69/month (each additional) on Pro and above. Included entitlements: Growth 1 (after qualification), Business 1 (a second is assigned where traffic justifies it), Enterprise Cloud up to 3 based on architecture. Inbox placement monitoring is an add-on at €99/month on any paid plan; EU-only routing is €149/month on Pro and above. Compliance (€990/month), Platform (€990/month base plus usage) and Mailboxes (€6 per seat/month, 10-seat minimum) are quoted on contract, see the contract lines above. Annual subscriptions are billed at 10× the monthly price, 16.7% less than twelve monthly payments. Enterprise requires an annual contract. All totals exclude VAT and contractual services. The next page shows the itemised estimate with the plan, included volume, overage and IP costs.
