Frequently Asked Questions

How does ApexMail pricing work?

We charge based on monthly email volume with clear plan limits. DKIM, SPF, DMARC, webhooks, and core analytics are included broadly; higher tiers add dedicated IPs, SSO, compliance workflows, and account coverage.

Is there a free tier?

Yes. The free tier includes 3,000 emails per month, and every new account gets a one-time 30,000-email launch allowance for its first 30 days. No credit card required. You can upgrade at any time as your volume grows.

What happens if I exceed my plan limit?

Extra volume beyond a paid plan is billed per plan: Developer €0.80, Pro €0.60, Growth/Business €0.35 per 1,000; Enterprise Cloud €0.35 per 1,000 (contractual). The Free plan has no automatic overage — upgrade instead. Usage, abuse, and payment controls continue to apply. Review your billing setup for the applicable limits and alerts.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes. Annual subscriptions are billed at 10 times the monthly price, which is a 16.7% saving versus twelve monthly payments. Enterprise annual contracts follow the signed order form.

Is HIPAA compliance included?

HIPAA availability is not currently offered. Enterprise can support compliance workflow discussions, but a regulated use case may proceed only after written confirmation and any required agreement with our security and legal teams.

Can I switch plans at any time?

Paid-plan changes use secure Adyen Checkout or the Adyen billing portal. A plan does not change until Adyen confirms the billing event and ApexMail reconciles the verified Adyen webhook. Enterprise annual contracts follow the signed order form and service terms.
