Billing cycle

Press Calculate — your estimate renders instantly on the next page using the billing service's live plan tables.

Plan boundaries are drawn from the active billing catalog: Free (3,000/mo), Developer (50K/mo), Pro (150K/mo), Growth (500K/mo), Business (2M/mo), Enterprise Cloud (5M/mo). Dedicated IPs are an add-on from €49/month (first) and €69/month (each additional) on Pro and above. Included entitlements: Growth 1 (after qualification), Business 1 (a second is assigned where traffic justifies it), Enterprise Cloud up to 3 based on architecture. Annual subscriptions are billed at 10× the monthly price. Enterprise requires an annual contract. All totals exclude VAT and contractual services.
