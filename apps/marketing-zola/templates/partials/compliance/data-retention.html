Data Retention Policy

How long we keep your data

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes it was collected. Retention windows per data category are summarized below; Enterprise contracts can extend event and email-content retention as described in your agreement.

ApexMail data retention periods by data category.
Data Category Retention Period
Account data Duration of contract + 30 days
Billing records 7 years (Estonian accounting law)
Email content 30 days (configurable up to 90 days for Enterprise)
Event logs 90 days (730 days for Enterprise)
Support tickets 2 years after resolution

Retention is enforced by automated job schedules; deletions are recorded in the audit trail. See our privacy policy for the data-subject rights that apply alongside these windows.
