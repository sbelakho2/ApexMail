Frequently Asked Questions

How does ApexMail pricing work?

We charge based on monthly email volume with clear plan limits. DKIM, SPF, DMARC, webhooks, and core analytics are included broadly; higher tiers add dedicated IPs, SSO, audit logs, compliance workflows, and account coverage.

Is there a free tier?

Yes. We offer a free tier of 30,000 emails per month. No credit card required. You can upgrade at any time as your volume grows.

What happens if I exceed my plan limit?

If you exceed your plan's email volume, subscription overage is billed at $0.40 per 1,000 extra emails while account, abuse, and payment controls remain in effect. You'll receive alerts at 80% and 100% of your limit.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes — annual billing is priced at 10 months, giving roughly a 17% discount compared to monthly. Contact our sales team for custom enterprise agreements.

Is HIPAA compliance included?

Enterprise includes a HIPAA BAA workflow, encryption controls, audit logging, and implementation review. We scope regulated use cases with your security and legal teams before production launch.

Can I switch plans at any time?

Self-serve plans can be upgraded or downgraded from the dashboard with prorated billing. Enterprise annual contracts follow the signed order form and service terms.
