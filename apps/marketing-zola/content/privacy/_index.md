## 1. Introduction

Bel Consulting OÜ (trading as ApexMail) ("we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard personal data when you use our services, in compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation) and the Estonian Personal Data Protection Act (Isikuandmete kaitse seadus).

## 2. Data We Collect

- **Website visitors (marketing site):** IP address, browser type and user agent, device information, pages visited, referring URL, and timestamps, collected automatically from server access logs. The marketing site runs no analytics scripts and embeds no tracking pixels or advertising cookies.
- **Prospects and business contacts:** name, employer and job title, business email address, business phone number (if known), professional social profile, company domain and industry, and our communication history. We receive these details when you provide them through a contact form, an email or a sales conversation, and we also obtain them from public business registers, company websites, and business-data and enrichment providers.
- **Account data:** name, email, company, billing address, VAT number, payment method tokens (card details are processed by Mollie and never reach our servers), API key metadata, login timestamps, IP addresses, and audit log entries.
- **Usage data:** API calls, email sending volumes, delivery events, and engagement events (opens and clicks) for messages you send.
- **Recipient data (as processor for our customers):** recipient email addresses and names, email content (subject, body, headers, attachments), and delivery and engagement metadata. When tracking is enabled by the sending customer, open and click events also record the recipient's IP address and user agent.
- **Technical data:** IP addresses (used for authentication, rate limiting, and fraud and abuse prevention), browser type, device information.
- **AI processing:** when you use optional AI-assisted features, the subject lines and message content you submit are processed to generate the requested analysis (for example, subject-line insights).

## 3. Prospects and Business Contacts

This section applies when we contact you as a business prospect rather than as a customer or website visitor. It supplements, and does not replace, the rest of this policy.

**Where the data comes from.** We obtain business contact data from public business registers, company websites (including the pages where a company presents its team), and business-data and enrichment providers. We may combine it with information you give us through a contact form, an email or a sales conversation. Contact details and machine-derived facts keep their source, the date they were collected, and, where available, the source document or page.

**What we collect.** Your name, employer and job title, business email address, business phone number where available, professional social profile, the company's domain and industry, and our communication history with you.

**Why we use it.** To identify companies and people who may need ApexMail's email delivery service, to personalize our outreach, to send relevant business emails, and to keep a record of the conversation. We do not use prospect data for automated decision-making with legal effects, and we do not sell it.

**Legal basis for this processing.** We rely on our legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR in marketing our services to businesses. We have carried out a balancing assessment: the data is limited to business contact details, the outreach is relevant to your professional role, its volume is controlled, and your rights and interests are weighed against ours. You can object at any time, and we honour the objection as explained below.

**How long we keep prospect data.** We keep prospect data while the business relationship is active or while it remains relevant to our outreach. We delete or anonymise records that are no longer needed. When you object to direct marketing, we stop using your details for that purpose and keep only the minimum suppression record needed to make sure we do not contact you again.

**Recipients (processors).** Our EU/EEA infrastructure provider hosts the sales database. Our configured email transport delivers the messages. The enrichment and discovery providers we use return business-contact data to us. Our AI inference runtime may assist with drafting when it is enabled. Each processor is bound by a data processing agreement and processes the data only on our instructions. The current list of subprocessors is published on our [Subprocessors](/subprocessors/) page.

**Your unconditional right to object to direct marketing.** You have the right to object at any time to the processing of your personal data for direct marketing, including profiling connected with direct marketing. This right is unconditional under Article 21(2) GDPR: we do not apply a balancing test, and we stop processing your data for direct marketing as soon as you object. Use the unsubscribe link in any email, or contact us at **privacy@apexmail.ee**. The objection does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the objection.

## 4. Legal Basis

We process data under GDPR Article 6(1)(b) (contract performance), Article 6(1)(f) (legitimate interest), and Article 6(1)(a) (consent) where applicable.

## 7. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights:

- **Right of access** (Art. 15), obtain confirmation of whether we process your data and access that data.
- **Right to rectification** (Art. 16), request correction of inaccurate data.
- **Right to erasure** (Art. 17), request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
- **Right to restriction of processing** (Art. 18), restrict processing under certain conditions.
- **Right to data portability** (Art. 20), receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Right to object** (Art. 21), object to processing based on legitimate interests, and object at any time and unconditionally to direct marketing, including profiling connected with direct marketing under Article 21(2) GDPR.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **privacy@apexmail.ee**. We will respond within one month.

California residents: see our [Do Not Sell My Personal Information](/privacy/do-not-sell/) notice, ApexMail does not sell personal information.
