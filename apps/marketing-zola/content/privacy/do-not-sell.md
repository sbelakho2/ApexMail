## Your Rights Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

ApexMail does **not** sell personal information. We have never sold personal data to third parties, and we never will.

Under the CCPA, California residents have the right to:

- **Right to Know** — Request information about the categories and specific pieces of personal data we collect, use, disclose, and share.
- **Right to Delete** — Request deletion of personal data we have collected from you.
- **Right to Opt-Out of Sale** — Direct a business that sells personal data to stop selling it.

## ApexMail's Position

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information for monetary consideration. This means:

- We do not share email addresses, names, or any personal data with advertisers, data brokers, or third-party data aggregators.
- Our revenue comes from paid subscriptions — not from selling or monetising user data.
- We have no plans to change this model.

## How We Use Your Data

We only process personal data to provide our email delivery services. This includes:

- Account management and authentication
- Email delivery and event tracking (opens, clicks, bounces)
- Analytics and performance reporting
- Compliance with legal obligations

Full details are available in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).

## Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of your CCPA rights, submit a request to:

- **Email:** privacy@apexmail.ee
- **Data Subject Access Request:** Data Subject Access Request form
- **Post:** Bel Consulting OÜ, Sakala 7-2, 10141 Tallinn, Estonia

We will verify your identity before processing any request. We will respond within 45 days as required by the CCPA.

## Non-Discrimination

We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights. We will not:

- Deny you services
- Charge different prices or rates
- Provide a different level or quality of services

## Authorised Agents

You may designate an authorised agent to submit a request on your behalf. We will require written proof of the agent's authorisation and may contact you directly to verify your identity.
