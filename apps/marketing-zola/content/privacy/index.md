## 1. Introduction

ApexMail OÜ ("we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard personal data when you use our services.

**Data Controller:** ApexMail OÜ, Tallinn, Estonia.

## 2. Data We Collect

- **Account data:** name, email, company, billing address.
- **Usage data:** API calls, email sending volumes, delivery events.
- **Technical data:** IP addresses, browser type, device information.

## 3. Legal Basis

We process data under GDPR Article 6(1)(b) (contract performance), Article 6(1)(f) (legitimate interest), and Article 6(1)(a) (consent) where applicable.

## 4. Data Storage

All data is stored in EU data centres (Tallinn, Estonia). We do not transfer data outside the EEA without adequate safeguards.

## 5. Data Retention

Account data is retained while your account is active plus 90 days. Email event data is retained for the period specified in your plan (30–365 days).

## 6. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, and object to processing of your data. Contact privacy@apexmail.ee.

See our [Cookie Policy](/cookies) for details on cookies and tracking.

For privacy inquiries: privacy@apexmail.ee
