## Enterprise Email

ApexMail Enterprise is the contractual option for organizations that need high-volume transactional email, procurement support, or a deployment review. It is not a generic self-service Checkout plan: activation follows an executed order form or other approved billing arrangement.

## When to Contact Sales

Enterprise may be appropriate when an organization needs one or more of the following:

- Volume above the self-service Business plan.
- A signed order form, purchase order, invoice, or negotiated payment terms.
- A security, architecture, data-residency, or deployment review.
- Private Cloud or Bring Your Own IP planning.
- A contractual SLA or dedicated account coverage.

The sales team will confirm the available deployment model, support terms, data-residency scope, and any professional services before contracting.

## Security and Compliance Position

- ApexMail supports GDPR-oriented data-processing workflows and provides a DPA.
- HIPAA availability is **not currently offered**. Do not send protected health information on the assumption that an Enterprise plan creates a HIPAA or BAA entitlement.
- ApexMail is **not currently SOC 2 certified**. Internal readiness work is not an included plan feature or a certification-date commitment.
- Security questionnaires and documentation are reviewed case by case. Any commitment is effective only when included in the signed agreement.

## Billing and Entitlement Lifecycle

Enterprise access is created through the approved contractual process. For self-service plans, a selected plan is only activated after Stripe confirms billing and ApexMail processes the verified subscription webhook. A Checkout completion page alone does not grant an entitlement.

Existing subscription changes and cancellations use the Stripe billing portal. Enterprise changes, renewals, and termination follow the signed contract.
