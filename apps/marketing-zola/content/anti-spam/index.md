## 1. Policy Statement

ApexMail strictly prohibits the use of its platform to send spam or unsolicited bulk email. This Anti-Spam Policy is part of our [Acceptable Use Policy](/acceptable-use/) and applies to all customers.

## 2. Permission Requirement

All recipients of email sent through ApexMail must have given **explicit, verifiable opt-in consent** to receive email from the sender. The following do not constitute consent:

- Purchased, scraped, or rented email lists.
- Email addresses harvested from public websites.
- Consent obtained through pre-checked boxes or bundled terms.
- Consent transferred from a third party without direct confirmation.

## 3. Sending Obligations

Every email sent through ApexMail must:

- Include a functional unsubscribe link that processes requests within 24 hours.
- Clearly identify the sender's name and physical address.
- Use a truthful and non-deceptive subject line.
- Match the content to the consent given (no bait-and-switch).
- Honor unsubscribe requests promptly and permanently.

## 4. Monitoring and Enforcement

ApexMail monitors bounce rates, complaint rates, and spam trap hits across all accounts. Accounts exceeding the following thresholds are subject to review and enforcement action:

| Metric | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Complaint rate | > 0.1% |
| Hard bounce rate | > 2% |
| Spam trap hits | Any |

### Enforcement Escalation

1. Warning and guidance for first-time minor violations.
2. Sending throttle for repeated issues.
3. Account suspension for serious or repeated violations.
4. Account termination for egregious violations (purchased lists, phishing, persistent non-compliance).

## 5. Reporting Spam

Report spam originating from ApexMail to [abuse@apexmail.ee](mailto:abuse@apexmail.ee). Include the full email headers and body. All reports are investigated within 48 hours.
