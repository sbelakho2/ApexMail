## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us provide a better experience.

## 2. Cookies We Use

### Essential Cookies
- **Session cookie:** Maintains your login state. Required for authenticated pages.
- **CSRF token:** Prevents cross-site request forgery attacks.

### Analytics Cookies
- **Plausible Analytics:** Privacy-friendly analytics. No personal data collected. No cookie consent required under GDPR.

### Preference Cookies
- **Theme preference:** Remembers light/dark mode selection.
- **Cookie consent:** Remembers your cookie preference choice.

## 3. Third-Party Cookies

We do not use third-party advertising cookies. We do not sell or share cookie data with third parties.

## 4. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may prevent the console from functioning.
