## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us provide a better experience. This policy explains what cookies we use and how you can control them, in compliance with the ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC) and the GDPR.

## 2. Cookies We Use

### Essential Cookies (Always Active)
- **Session cookie:** Maintains your login state. Required for authenticated pages. No consent required.
- **CSRF token:** Prevents cross-site request forgery attacks. No consent required.

### Analytics (Optional)
- **Plausible Analytics:** Lightweight, privacy-friendly analytics. Plausible does not use persistent cookies and does not collect any personal data. While Plausible is GDPR-compliant without requiring consent, we give you the choice to opt out of the analytics pixel as a best-practice transparency measure.

### Preference Cookies
- **Theme preference:** Remembers light/dark mode selection. Set based on your explicit choice.
- **Cookie consent:** Remembers your cookie preference choice. Set when you interact with the cookie banner.

## 3. Third-Party Cookies

We do not use third-party advertising cookies. We do not sell or share cookie data with third parties. Our infrastructure sub-processors (Hetzner Online GmbH) may set strictly necessary cookies for load-balancing sessions.

## 4. Your Choices

When you first visit our site, a cookie banner gives you the following options:

- **Accept All:** Essential cookies + analytics pixel enabled.
- **Necessary Only:** Only essential cookies enabled. Analytics pixel is blocked.
- **Reject All:** Same as Necessary Only — only essential cookies.

You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser cookies and reloading the page.

## 5. Managing Cookies via Browser

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:
- View cookies stored on your device.
- Block all or third-party cookies.
- Delete individual or all cookies.

Disabling essential cookies may prevent the ApexMail console from functioning correctly.
