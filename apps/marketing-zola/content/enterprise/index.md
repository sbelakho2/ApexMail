## Who Enterprise Is For

ApexMail Enterprise is designed for organizations that need:

- High-volume transactional email (typically 5,000,000+ emails/month)
- Custom contractual terms beyond online terms of service
- Procurement-driven buying processes
- Security, compliance, and data residency requirements
- Dedicated or private deployment options
- SLA-backed support with dedicated account management

## Qualification Criteria

Enterprise is the right fit when your organization requires at least one of:

- Volume exceeding 5,000,000 emails/month with custom overage rates
- Custom SLA (99.95%+ with credits)
- Dedicated tenancy or BYOC deployment
- Custom data residency requirements
- Contractual terms beyond standard online terms
- DPA, BAA, or other compliance documentation
- Procurement process (PO, invoice, wire transfer)
- Dedicated account management and support

## Private Cloud Relationship

Enterprise and Private Cloud are separate but related offerings:

- **Enterprise (shared cloud)**: Multi-tenant infrastructure with enterprise-grade features, SLA, and support. Included in the Enterprise starting price.
- **Private Cloud (Dedicated Tenant)**: Single-tenant dedicated infrastructure. Additional from EUR 4,000/mo.
- **Private Cloud (BYOC)**: Customer-cloud deployment. Additional from EUR 6,500/mo.

Customers may use Enterprise shared cloud, then transition to Private Cloud as needs evolve.
