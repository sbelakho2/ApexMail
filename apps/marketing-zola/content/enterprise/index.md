## ApexMail Enterprise

Enterprise is a contractual email-delivery offering for organizations that need a commercial, security, or deployment review beyond the public self-service plans.

## Catalog Entitlement

| Item | Enterprise |
|---|---|
| Price | from €1,750/month under an annual contract |
| Included email volume | 5,000,000 emails/month |
| API calls | Unlimited |
| Domains and team members | Unlimited |
| Event retention | 730 days |
| Dedicated IPs | 10 included |
| Access controls | SAML SSO and audit logs |
| Support level | Dedicated |
| Private Cloud / BYOIP | Subject to contract and technical review |

The catalog price is from €1,750/month (€17,500/year at 10 months for 12). Custom volume, deployment, and professional-service costs are negotiated and stated only in a written proposal or order form.

## Procurement Review

A qualified buyer can request the current DPA, subprocessor information, security overview, architecture material, and applicable service terms. Invoice, purchase-order, or alternative payment terms are available only where approved in the contract.

## Security and Compliance

- ApexMail supports GDPR-oriented processing documentation and offers a DPA for review.
- HIPAA availability and a BAA are **not currently offered**.
- Any SLA, data-residency scope, security questionnaire response, or private-deployment commitment is controlled by the signed agreement.

## Billing Lifecycle

Enterprise is not purchasable through generic public Checkout. The contract or approved billing arrangement is the entitlement authority. For the public self-service plans, Stripe Checkout and the verified Stripe subscription webhook are required before a paid plan is granted.
