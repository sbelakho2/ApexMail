## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines prohibited and acceptable uses of the ApexMail email infrastructure. Violation of this policy may result in suspension or termination.

## 2. Prohibited Content

You may not use ApexMail to send:

- **Spam:** Unsolicited bulk email without prior opt-in consent.
- **Phishing:** Emails designed to fraudulently obtain personal information.
- **Malware:** Emails containing viruses, trojans, or malicious attachments.
- **Illegal content:** Content that violates applicable laws in Estonia, the EU, or the recipient's jurisdiction.
- **Harassment:** Threatening, abusive, or defamatory content.

## 3. Sending Requirements

- All recipients must have given explicit opt-in consent.
- Every email must include a working unsubscribe mechanism.
- Sender identity must be clearly stated (no spoofing).
- Bounce and complaint rates must remain below 2% and 0.1% respectively.

## 4. Infrastructure Protection

- Do not attempt to bypass rate limits or quotas.
- Do not use the service for DDoS attacks or network abuse.
- Do not share API keys or credentials.

## 5. Enforcement

We monitor sending patterns and will:
1. Warn for first-time minor violations.
2. Throttle sending for repeated issues.
3. Suspend accounts for serious or repeated violations.
4. Terminate accounts for egregious violations.

## 6. Reporting

Report abuse to: abuse@apexmail.ee
