IBM PLEX MONO / TECHNICAL METADATA

Ink on Paper reads at 17.45:1, Forest at 11.04:1, Muted at 5.66:1, Sage on Ink at 7.69:1, Teal on Ink at 12.74:1 and Teal Deep on Paper at 7.13:1. Teal on Paper is a decorative accent only (1.37:1). Brand colors and outcome semantics stay distinct, and state meaning is carried by more than color alone.

Keep the master intact. H/4 clear space on all sides of the visible artwork; empty viewBox padding does not count. Every master carries currentColor, Ink, white and accent variants through its color; no stretching, rotation, letter-spacing changes, cropping, outlining, gradients, shadows or added ornament. The outlined lettering is fixed artwork (kiwi at 100 units, captcha at 82 on the same baseline — design/lettering.json); the reading fonts are self-hosted Alegreya Sans and IBM Plex Mono under the SIL Open Font License.
